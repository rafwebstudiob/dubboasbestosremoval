Asbestos removal in Narromine

Narromine is about 40 km west of Dubbo by road, in Narromine Shire. Here's what to know about asbestos in local homes and farm buildings, and where it can go.

Last checked 24 October 2026

Asbestos around Narromine

Narromine is surrounded by irrigated and dryland farms, so a lot of asbestos jobs are asbestos-cement roofs and walls on sheds, plus fibro in older homes in town. Shed roofs are almost always more than 10 m², which makes them licensed work.

A different council

Narromine is in Narromine Shire Council, not Dubbo Regional Council, so the local disposal option is different.

Narromine Shire Council's Narromine Waste Management Facility is on Gainsborough Road. Council lists it as open Sunday, Monday, Wednesday, Friday and Saturday, 9am to 4pm (gates shut at 3:45pm), closed Tuesday, Thursday and every public holiday. The NSW EPA lists it as accepting household asbestos; phone (02) 6889 9950 first.

Who can remove it

In NSW, SafeWork NSW says a licensed professional must remove any friable asbestos and any amount of bonded (non-friable) asbestos over 10 m², and it recommends a licensed professional for any amount.
